Meet Bramble · $72

Bramble isn't feeling great. He gets it.

Navy-and-tangerine stripes, slate ankle boots, and a few bald patches. Bramble's fur is thinning, his skin gets red and flaky, his eyes are sore and teary, and his wings have gone a little crumpled. His lips drip, too (clear rubbery plastic, sculpted by hand).

If Bramble were a real bug, he'd need a doctor. Since he's felt, he has a different job: keeping company with kids who know what it's like. Itchy skin, hair that falls out, eyes that won't stop watering, long days in waiting rooms, feeling like everyone's looking. Bramble gets it, and he doesn't mind being looked at.

  • Itchy, flaky skin
  • Thin patches in his fur
  • Sore, teary eyes
  • Crumpled wings
  • Still standing on all six

How a Thang is made

From sketch to shelf in four steps.

Sketch

Every character starts as a pencil drawing pinned above the felting table.

Felt

Raw wool is stabbed into shape by hand: body, snout, eye orbs, then the long mossy fur.

Dress

Knit stalks and legs, upcycled wings with blanket-stitch trim, and tiny lace-up boots.

Settle

Before it ships, each Thang spends one night alone in the studio with the lights off. We find this calms them down.

It's evening where you are. A perfect time to get cozy. Muffi usually wakes up around 3:12.

Fan favourite · Doza · $64

Doza comes pre-chewed.

In 1995 the studio dog got hold of the very first Doza and had himself a party: torn wings, a bald spot, yarn guts hanging out, and a chicken bone wedged right in his side (you can still make out the little ankle knob). Kids loved him even more after that, so now every Doza is made the same way.

All the gross stuff is fake and bone-dry: sculpted resin slobber, yarn innards, a felt bone. Fully lickable by dogs. Not recommended.

Pillo · $64

Pillo is so tired.

Pillo used to dream out loud. Lately Pillo doesn't feel like doing much of anything. Getting up is hard, food doesn't taste like much, and the things that used to be fun just… aren't. Pillo's body has gotten heavier, the knit eyestalks don't have wires anymore so they sag, and the snout is stitched into a frown that won't come undone.

Look closely and you'll find a few red stitches across Pillo's legs: old scars, sewn shut. And Pillo stopped taking the medicine that was helping, without telling anyone. (Pillo, please don't do that. If you're on medication, talk to your doctor before you stop.)

Pillo will probably sleep all day again. That's okay. Sit with them anyway. Pillo is for anyone who knows this kind of tired.

  • Heavier body
  • Soft stalks, no wires
  • Iridescent printed wings
  • Stitched-on frown
  • Sleeps a lot
  • Worth sitting with

Care

Caring for your Thang

  1. Spot clean with cool water. Never machine wash.
  2. Keep out of direct sun so the wool doesn't fade.
  3. Pose gently. The eyestalks will hold their shape.
  4. Don't wake it. Thangs sleep about twenty hours a day.
  5. If its eyes are open, do not look at the eyes.
  6. It prefers to face you. Let it.
  7. Never put it back in the box.

FAQ

Are Buggy-Thangs toys?

They're art collectibles for teens and grown-ups, with small felt parts, so keep them away from little kids. They don't like little kids.

Why does my Thang feel warm?

Wool holds heat really well. It's holding yours.

Can I wash it?

Spot clean only. Do not submerge. It remembers.

My Thang's eyes are open. Is that a defect?

The eyelids are hand-set half closed. Please send us a photo. Please don't stay in the room with it while you take the photo.

I only ordered one. Why did two arrive?

Sometimes they follow one home.
