Care guide

Looking after your Thang

Buggy-Thangs are handmade from wool, knit and upcycled textiles. A little care keeps them fuzzy for years.

Unboxing

Lift your Thang out by the body, never by the eyestalks. Fluff the moss fur with your fingertips; it gets a little flat in transit. Gently bend each leg until all six boots sit flat.

Keep the tissue paper. Most collectors like to save the box.

Posing

The eyestalks and legs are wired, so they'll hold whatever pose you give them. Tilt the eyes toward each other for a curious look, or let them droop for a proper nap.

Cleaning

Spot clean with a damp cloth and cool water. Never machine wash, never submerge, never use a dryer. Felt shrinks. Thangs don't like to shrink.

Storage

Display your Thang on an open shelf. They don't do well shut in a drawer or a closet. They get restless in the dark.

Never put it back in the box. We know we said keep the box. Keep it flat. Keep it empty.

At night

Before bed, turn your Thang to face the wall. This isn't required. Customers tell us it helps them fall asleep.

In the morning, if it's facing you again, don't mention it. It's sensitive about it.

If you hear soft steps on the shelf, that's the wool settling. Wool settles a lot. Wool settles across the room.

It's right now. You'll want to read the next part before tonight.

If it wakes

If it wakes

  1. Don't run. It has six legs.
  2. Don't turn on the light. It already knows what you look like.
  3. Don't look at the eyes. Look at the boots. The boots won't hurt you.
  4. Don't say its name. It doesn't think that's its name.
  5. If it's at the foot of the bed, keep your feet under the blanket.
  6. If it's on the pillow, it's too late for this list.
